Ultimate tic tac toe

  1. Nine boards, one grid. Every square of the big grid holds a whole tic tac toe board of its own.
  2. Your move sends me somewhere. Play the top-right square of any mini board and I must answer in the top-right mini board. The board you are sent to is outlined and everything else dims.
  3. Closed board? Free move. If the board you are sent to is already won or full, you may play in any open board.
  4. Win a mini board, claim its square. Three claimed squares in a row on the big grid wins the game.
  5. Drawn mini boards belong to nobody — they are closed, but neither player can claim them.
  6. Full grid, no line? Whoever won more mini boards takes it; equal counts is a draw.

Keyboard — arrows move inside a mini board, shift + arrows jump a whole board, enter plays. U undo, Y redo, R new game, M sound.
